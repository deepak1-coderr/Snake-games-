Pixel Snake is the classic snake game with a retro handheld look.

Swipe or use the on-screen pad to steer your snake. Every apple makes it longer. Hit the border or your own tail and the run is over.

FEATURES
- The snake grows with every apple you eat
- A solid border around the board
- Levels: every 5 apples takes you up a level
- Speed rises with each level, up to 2.5x
- Rocks appear from level 3 and block your path
- Score counts 10 points per apple, multiplied by your level
- Your best score is saved on your phone
- Three snake types: Classic, Hollow and Striped
- Light and dark look that follows your phone
- Works fully offline. No ads, no sign-up, no tracking
