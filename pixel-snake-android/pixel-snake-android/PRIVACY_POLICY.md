# Privacy Policy for Pixel Snake

Last updated: November 2, 2026

Pixel Snake is an offline game. It does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal information.

**What stays on your device.** The game saves your best score and your chosen snake type on your phone so they are there next time you play. This data never leaves your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its storage.

**What the game does not do.** It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no network access. It does not ask for any permissions such as location, contacts, camera or microphone.

**Children.** The game collects no data from anyone, including children.

**Changes.** If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at the same address with a new date.
